Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 14, 2026

Welcome to CoreFlow. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application (CoreFlow) and the associated backend APIs hosted on Vercel.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a secure and functional experience for managing meetings, organizational operations, and invoices. This includes:

  • Personal Authentication Data: Email address, full name, and avatar images provided during the login/registration process via Supabase Auth and Manus OAuth.
  • Application Activity Data: Meeting schedules, descriptions, organization structure, invoice details (including client name, tax details, and payment receipts).
  • System & Device Information: Standard request logs, including IP address, operating system, request headers, and device IDs to ensure app security and prevent unauthorized access.

2. How We Use Your Information

CoreFlow uses your data for the following essential business purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain secure user sessions.
  • To synchronize your meetings, schedules, and invoice data across multiple devices.
  • To enforce Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring users only access data they are authorized to view.
  • To diagnose system errors and improve app performance.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the application:

  • Supabase: For cloud hosting, PostgreSQL database storage, and secure user authentication.
  • Vercel: For executing and securing the API routing and serverless middleware.

4. Data Retention & Security

All personal and business data is stored using industry-standard encryption protocols on Supabase. We retain your data as long as your account remains active. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, edit, export, or delete your personal data. You can manage these settings directly in the app or contact support to request complete removal of your records.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@coreflow.app.
